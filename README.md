# CareerAI — AI Career Recommendation System
### 8th Science & Technology Exhibition 2026

A modern, sleek, multi-page AI career assessment and prediction platform.

---

## 🌟 Web Architecture

1. **Website 1: Landing Page (`index.html`)**:
   - Grand immersive Hero section with live status pill.
   - Prominent **"Begin Career Test Now →"** button linking to the assessment form.
   - System highlights and 3-step feature pipeline.

2. **Website 2: Form Page (`form.html`)**:
   - Heading: **"Know Your Career"**
   - Clean, centered, orderly 6-step questionnaire wizard:
     1. 📚 *Favourite Subjects* (up to 2 choices)
     2. 🔬 *Core Interests* (up to 2 choices)
     3. ⚡ *Natural Strengths* (up to 2 choices)
     4. 🏢 *Ideal Workplace* (up to 2 choices)
     5. 🧘 *Work Style & Rhythm* (up to 2 choices)
     6. 🏆 *Core Life Goal* (up to 2 choices)
   - Final Step Button: **"🚀 Predict My Career →"** (saves profile and navigates to the dedicated results page).

3. **Website 3: Dedicated Results Output Page (`results.html`)**:
   - Calibrated **99% Career Chance Score** for top match!
   - Shows:
     - 🥇 **#1 Top Predicted Career: Biotechnology Specialist (99% Chance)**
     - Estimated Salary Band ($85K - $145K / yr)
     - 10-Year Growth Outlook (+18% Very High)
     - Recommended Degree Pathway (B.Tech / B.Sc → M.Sc / Ph.D)
     - Key National Entrance Exams (CUET, GATE, JEE, NEET)
     - AI Rationale explaining why this career fits the student
     - Other Top Matches (Runners-up with clickable detail modals)
   - **"← Edit Preferences & Retake"** & **"📄 Print Career Report"** buttons.
